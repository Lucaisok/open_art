# Collectif Bruit Blanc

We are a theatre collective of three artists, founded in 2015 in Marseille, France.
We make site-specific performances with non-professional performers.

## Productions

- 2025: La Digue, Théâtre de la Criée, Marseille
- 2022: Les Voix du Port, festival Actoral, Marseille
- 2016: Premier Souffle, Friche la Belle de Mai, Marseille

## Support

- 2023: Région Sud creation grant
- 2019: Residency at La Chartreuse, Villeneuve-lès-Avignon
